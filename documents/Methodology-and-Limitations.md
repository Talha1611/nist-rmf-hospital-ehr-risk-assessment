# Methodology and Limitations

## Purpose
This note explains the assumptions used in the simulated Hospital EHR risk-management project.

## Method
The project used guided scenarios to practice:
- defining risk statements;
- assessing likelihood and impact;
- choosing and documenting risk treatment;
- tracking remediation and interim safeguards;
- identifying owners and evidence;
- handling exceptions and overdue actions;
- verifying control operation;
- reassessing residual risk and determining closure readiness.

## Risk scoring
High/Moderate/Low ratings and the sample RTO/RPO values used in the learning scenarios are illustrative only. They are not taken from a real hospital's risk appetite, service-level requirements, or policy. A real assessment should apply the organization's approved definitions, scoring matrix, risk acceptance criteria, and escalation thresholds.

## Evidence
No real hospital systems, user accounts, logs, backup repositories, tickets, or vulnerability scans were accessed. All scenario facts are hypothetical. Evidence listed in the workbook is evidence that would be required in a real assessment, not evidence actually collected.

## RMF alignment
The exercise applies practical concepts related to risk assessment, risk response, ongoing monitoring, remediation tracking, and authorization governance. It is not a complete formal NIST RMF implementation and does not create a formal authorization package or authorization decision.

## Responsible use
Do not present this simulated exercise as client work, an actual hospital assessment, or a certification. Describe it as a self-directed portfolio project demonstrating learning and applied reasoning.
